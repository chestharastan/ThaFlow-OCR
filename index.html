Before you start

Everything runs on your device. ThaOCR has no server, no account and no database. Your photos and scans are never uploaded. The OCR runtime and PDF reader (ONNX Runtime and pdf.js, from the jsDelivr CDN) and the models (about 40 MB, from Hugging Face where they are published openly) are downloaded once and kept in your browser. No document data is part of that download.

We do not store your files for you. Results stay only in this browser's local storage, on this device. Clearing the browser's site data, using a private window, or switching devices means they are gone. Export what you want to keep as Word, text or JSON.

Speed depends on your device. The page checks what your computer or phone can do, picks the model set that fits, and shows a time estimate before you start.
